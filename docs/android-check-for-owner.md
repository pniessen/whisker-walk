# Android check: 10 minutes on your phone

Chrome on any Android 9 or newer. The cat should survive all of this; nothing here deletes anything.

Address: https://pniessen.github.io/whisker-walk/ (use the whiskerwalk.org address once it is live).

## Add it to the Home screen
1. Open the address in Chrome (not Samsung Internet or Firefox for this first check).
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen, then Install).
4. Confirm. A cat-face icon appears on the Home screen and in the app drawer.
5. Look at the icon: is the cat whole, or is it cut off by the round or square mask your phone uses?

## The checks
1. (1 min) Chrome menu, Settings, About Chrome: note Android version, Chrome version, phone model. Open chrome://gpu and copy the renderer line and whether WebGL says Hardware accelerated.
2. (2 min) In the browser, make a cat and walk the Neighborhood. Note touch controls, button sizes, and whether sound starts after the first tap.
3. (2 min) Add ?perf to the address, for example https://pniessen.github.io/whisker-walk/?perf. Read the fps and cpu numbers after a minute. Then try ?rung=2&perf and ?rung=4&perf. Note which rung Auto picked.
4. (2 min) Open the game from the new Home screen icon. Does it fill the whole screen with no address bar? Then turn on airplane mode, close the app from the recent apps list, and open it again. Does it start? Can you walk into the Farm and the House?
5. (1 min) Rotate to portrait and back. Pull down the notification shade. Switch to another app for 30 seconds and return. Are sound, picture and the walk still there?
6. (1 min) Menu, Save: note the cloud code. Force-stop the app (long-press the icon, App info, Force stop) and reopen: is the cat still there?
7. (1 min) If a Bluetooth game pad is handy: press a button, walk with the stick, open the map.

## Send back
Phone model, Android and Chrome versions, fps at HIGH and the rung Auto picked, whether the installed app was fullscreen and worked offline, how the icon looks, and any black screen, sound dropout or lost cat.
