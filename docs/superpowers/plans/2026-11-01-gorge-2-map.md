# The Gorge, part 2: the Map Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Gorge as the fifth Clan Edition territory: a deep, shaded gorge floor with a stream, a winding path cut into the east wall with stops on the way, a bright clifftop with a view down the whole gorge, birds and a mountain goat, the Moor's border post opening onto it after 2 Moor walks, and CliffClan still "coming soon".

**Architecture:**
- The whole map is authored once, as data, in a pure module `src/world/gorgeshape.js` (no THREE). It lists walkable shapes (floor ledges, path legs, lips, the clifftop, cave and den floors) and rock masses. Masses are cut round every opening by rectangle subtraction, so the ground model never needs a "carve" shape.
- That one record feeds both `createGround` (part 1's engine) and a new builder piece, `rockTerrain`, which draws the rock and ledges from the same shapes. So a rim the cat stops at and the rock she sees can never disagree.
- `src/world/gorge.js` builds the area the way `pines.js` does: content records first, `GORGE_KEEP_CLEAR` built from them, seeded scenery, one wadeable stream with stepping stones, and a tall rock-tinted horizon.
- A small per-walk hook, `areaData.life(ctx)` → `{ update, dispose }`, carries what only the Gorge does at run time: shade by height, wheeling birds, and the ambience mix. Part 3 grows it for the caves.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS (ES modules), Three.js 0.185, Vite 8, Vitest 4. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-gorge-design.md`: owner decisions 1–11, "The map", "Everything else a territory has", "Builder-check rulings", "How it is split" item 2, and the Constraints. Part 1 is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-gorge-1-ground-height.md`; its rulings R1–R15 are assumed throughout. The caves' experience (darkness, glow, camera inside, interiors, the cave cat) is part 3: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-gorge-3-caves.md`.

**Why two plans, not one.** This plan authors every cave and den opening, because masses must be cut round them and the flood, keep-clear and stray rules must see them. Part 3 changes no shape. Part 3 is its own runtime system: light that dims with depth, a glow light, cave camera framing, interior culling, a resident cat and a rain shelter. It carries its own "never scary" risk and its own live check, and needs its own review gate. Together the two would be about 19 tasks, twice the Pines build.

## Global Constraints

- **Nothing changes elsewhere, with four named exceptions.**
  - The Moor gains a trail to its east post. `MOOR_GORGE_TRAIL` is filtered last, as `MOOR_TRAIL` is, so only the trees and beds on the trail move. The reviewer measured one heather and one gorse, so the Moor's triangle count moves a little: re-measure it in the budget comment.
  - The Moor's post to the Gorge changes from "not explored yet" to locked or open.
  - The clan picker shows CliffClan as a card that can't be picked yet.
  - Four seeded or random spots on shipped maps go through the new `onlyOn` and level passes (G7). These are no-ops wherever there is no `onlyOn` and the ground is flat, which is every shipped map.
  - Everything else holds: part 1's `test/flattrace.test.js` stays byte-identical at every commit, the golden fixtures are never regenerated, and every shipped area's `test/budget.test.js` numbers stay where they are.
- `SAVE_VERSION` stays 4. The only new save content is the `gorge` key in `walks` and `marks`, the `'gorge'` unlock id, and the `'goat'` journal type, all additive and defaulted.
- The catalog record is `gorge: { name: 'The Gorge', price: 0, requires: { area: 'moor', walks: 2 }, edition: 'canon' }` (owner decision 10). There is no post from Twolegplace, and the Neighborhood is unchanged.
- `src/world/gorge.js` and `src/world/gorgeshape.js` never read the edition: no `/edition/i` anywhere in either file, comments included. Nothing on the wire changes.
- **Determinism.**
  - No new draw on `walkRng` or `strayStream`.
  - Every seeded scatter is seeded by name (`seedFromCode`), and every candidate draws all its numbers whether or not it is kept.
  - Heights come from map data only.
- **One walkable height per spot.** Nothing walkable lies above anything walkable. Caves and dens go into rock, and the rock above them is never reached. This is proven by the flood in Task 2.
- **Edges stop her.** Every walkable edge with a drop of 1.5 m or more has a visible lip: a strip 0.4 m wide and 0.35 m above the walkable ground beside it. So the cat stops at a low rock wall she can see, and her body never hangs over a drop. Drops of under 1.5 m do not occur on this map.
- **Slopes** are no steeper than 1 in 3, and `validateGround` checks it. The legs here are gentler than 1 in 8.
- **No CliffClan.** There is no camp, no cast, no intruder route and no `HOMES.gorge` change; that is all part 4. The camp's part of the Wide Ledge (`[5.8,20,35.5,48]`) and its den are kept free of every find, ring, POI, prop and scenery item, and are tagged `reserved: 'part 4'`. The leg B → C turn (z 32–35.5) stays outside the reserve, so no climb has to cross the camp.
- **Our own words.** Every new label is plain or newly made. None contains a scanned word (`kit`, `apprentice`, `warrior`, `elder`, `queen`, `medicine cat`, `deputy`, `leader`, `loner`, `rogue`, `kittypet`, `Twolegplace`, `Clan`) or a book place name. Task 4 holds a test over every label with `/gathering|four ?trees|moon|high ?stones|high ?rock|high ?ledge|mother ?mouth|sunning|snake ?rocks|tall ?pines|rock ?pile|sky ?oak|hollow|rushing water/i`.
- **Budget:** the Cozy Neighborhood's. As built, at most 450 meshes, 380 casters and 60,000 triangles; merged, 260 meshes and 205 casters. The rock alone is held to 15,000 triangles (Task 3). The live checks are 60 fps on the iPhone 18 (HIGH) on the floor and on the clifftop looking down (owner step 3), and a desktop `?perf` reading at the same spots (Task 12).
- **Commits:** explicit `git add <paths>`, never `-A` or `.`. Every message ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Never merge to main and never push. Never delete a file you didn't create, and never use wildcards in `rm`. Don't touch other worktrees.
- **Tests:** `npx vitest run --exclude '.claude/**' --exclude '.worktrees/**'`. Build: `npm run build`. Run `npm ci` first if `node_modules` is missing.

## Rulings (the planner's calls, 2026-11-01; each with what it costs if wrong)

- **G1. The frame.** The Gorge sits directly east of the Moor in the same z frame: a Moor point (x, z) is the Gorge point (x − 100, z). Its west edge faces the Moor, where the north path runs past. Its south edge faces Twolegplace, which the gorge walls hide; the horizon shows rooftops there. North and east are more mountains.
  - The playtester's board puts the Gorge south-east of the Moor (815, 353 against 620, 161). This frame simplifies that, and the Moor's shipped east-edge post already commits to it.
  - Cost if wrong: horizon tints and one post's z. Low.
- **G2. Carving by subtraction, not a new ground shape.** All openings (path legs, ledges, caves, dens) are axis-aligned rectangles. `subtractRects(mass, openings)` cuts each mass into the rectangles left over, and those become ordinary flat strips, so `src/ground.js` gains no shape kind.
  - Cost if wrong: if part 3 or part 4 wants a cave that isn't axis-aligned, a `carves` list in `createGround` has to be added after all.
- **G3. Lips as ground.** A lip is a 0.4 m strip at walkable height + 0.35. That is above `STEP_MAX` plus a sub-step's grade (0.245), so it is a wall both ways. It is drawn as a low rock band. It stops corner-cutting between stacked legs too: the flood finds no step from one leg to the next except at the hairpins.
  - Cost if wrong: if a lip reads as clutter, it is drawn thinner. The ground stays.
- **G4. The map is drawn from its own ground record.** `rockTerrain(spec)` draws only **exposed** faces.
  - A side face is drawn only over the height range where the ground just outside it is lower than the shape's top. It is drawn down to that lower ground, or to 0.
  - Faces on the ±62 outer edges are not drawn.
  - Mass tops are drawn, because the highest camera sees over the rims (G6).
  - Walkable raised shapes get their tops (ramps sloped) and their exposed sides.
  - **A lip is a column** from the lower neighbour's ground (or 0) up to its top, so no slot ever opens between legs.
  - Each ceiling cap is a box from the ceiling up to the mass top over it.
  - All of it goes into a few merged meshes. Faces stay exactly on the shape edges, with no outward jitter. Rock texture is vertex-colour strata by height plus `surfaceProps('stone')`.
  - Cost if wrong: if the rock looks boxy on the iPhone, a later visual pass adds inward-only chamfers and crag props. No shape changes.
- **G5. Shade without a sun clock.** There are three parts:
  - the rock's baked vertex colours run from cool and dark at y 0 (`ROCK_LOW 0x59606c`) to warm at the top (`ROCK_HIGH 0xc4b08e`), so the look-down reads shaded;
  - the floor's ground plane is a darker, cooler colour than any other map's (`0x5f5d52`). So the floor reads shaded from the clifftop even beyond the shadow map's reach, where the west wall's real shadow (the sun is at 19° from the north-west) stops;
  - the clifftop's features (the Summit Rock, the view scenic, the finish ring and the Glitter Cave's mouth) sit south of z −27. North of that, the 20 m north rim shades the clifftop in the afternoon light;
  - `gorgeLife` scales the sun by `shadeFor(groundY)` (0.6 on the floor, rising to 1.0 by y 10, on a smoothstep from 2 to 10, eased at 1.5/s) and the fill by `0.85 + 0.15 · shade`.
  - The sun and fill are composed with weather's own writes through `composeIntensity` (Task 8), so a rainbow or dusk value is never lost.
  - Cost if wrong: if the floor reads too dark, `SHADE_FLOOR` is one constant.
- **G6. Heights.**
  - `FLOOR 0`, `LOOKOUT 4`, `WIDE_LEDGE 8`, `CLIFFTOP 12`.
  - Rock: `WEST_TOP 9` (the gorge's west wall, lower than the clifftop so the view goes over it), `SHOULDER 14`, and `RIM 20` on every mass at the bounds.
  - **The highest camera is far above the rims.** `catcam.cameraOffset` gives `height + dist · sin(pitch) · 0.9`. At zoom step 3 and the player's top pitch (0.85) that is 13.94 m over the cat. On the Summit Rock (12 + 1.9) that makes `CAM_TOP` ≈ 27.9 m. Compute it in the tests from `cameraOffset`, `ZOOM_STEPS` and the pitch clamp, never from a literal.
  - So rims block the view at ordinary framing: 20 m is above the clifftop camera at zoom step 1 and the default pitch. From higher up she sees over them, onto rock tops drawn as rock (G4) and the 40 m horizon band beyond.
  - The fog (40–130 m) does the rest. The test that matters is that every downward look (−15° or lower) from `CAM_TOP` lands on rock, ground or the band (Task 4).
  - Cost if wrong: if a rim top looks bare from high up, scree and stand pines go onto the rim tops; no shape changes.
- **G7. Strays, ghosts and every random spot stand only where a cat can walk.**
  - `area.keepOut.onlyOn: Rect[]` (new in `src/keepout.js`): `keepOutPoint` moves a point that is in none of the rectangles to the nearest point inside the nearest one, with the margin.
  - `onlyOn` also applies inside `dryGroundPoint`'s `isClear` test and its ring search, not just at the first push.
  - Scent treats keep their POI's level: a treat whose ground differs from its POI's by more than `LEVEL_TOL` goes back to the POI's own spot.
  - These go through `keepOutPoint` after their draws, so no draw moves: the walk's rain puddles (`walk.js` ~638, walkRng spots within ±25 m), the dusk fireflies (`critters.js` ~254) and the unicorn's flee target (`secrets.js` ~122).
  - With no `onlyOn`, or on flat ground, every point is untouched.
  - Cost if wrong: if a system that picks spots is missed, a treat or a stray stands on rock. Task 10's sweep over 200 seeds catches it.
- **G8. The climb race.** `areaData.raceCourse` is a fixed, ordered course: five rings from the floor up the path to a clifftop finish (spec, builder-check fix). `createRace` gains `{ kind: 'climb' }` for the pad's prompt, and a ring counts only when `ground.sameLevel(cat.x, cat.z, ring.x, ring.z)`. That gate is always true on flat ground, so other races are unchanged.
  - Cost if wrong: a random course here sends kids up and down the path; that is the spec's own reason for the ruling.
- **G9. The goat** is a critter that only bolts. It isn't in `STALKABLE`, `PREY` or `CHASEABLE`, so it can't be stalked, pounced or Far-Called.
  - It lives on **the shoulder**, the 14 m crag between the Lookout and the clifftop: 2 m above the clifftop and leg C, so she sees it against the sky from both, and from the floor. A goat on a 20 m rim would be hidden by the rim's edge, and 8 m above her it could never notice her in 3D.
  - It notices her at `GOAT_NOTICE 6` m, **measured in 2D**. Critters measure in 3D today (`critters.js` ~452).
  - It flees at 7 m/s with bounds of `0.25 · |sin(9t)|`, and hides on the shoulder.
  - **Owner answer (2026-11-01): only on some days.** `GOAT_DAY_SHARE = 0.5`. `goatToday(date)` is a pure function of the walk date: one draw from `mulberry32(seedFromCode(`${date}-gorge-goat`))` below the share. It never touches walkRng or strayStream, so both devices agree. On a no-goat day walk.js drops the goat from `critterSpawns` and the `goat-leap` moment; its hide stays (nothing else uses it).
  - Fleeing critters pick the nearest hide **on their own level**, `ground.sameLevel(c.x, c.z, h.x, h.z)`, so the goat never runs at the floor's hides.
  - Cost if wrong: the share is one constant.
- **G10. CliffClan in the picker.**
  - `clanPickerModel` lists every `HOMES` clan. A clan that isn't `pickable` shows as a card with `aria-disabled="true"`, no `PICK_ATTR` and the line `camp coming soon`. Owner decision 11 and its "staying 'territory coming soon'" wording assumed it showed. Today it doesn't: `HOMES.gorge` has `pickable: false`, so it is hidden.
  - "Territory" would be wrong once the Gorge is walkable, hence "camp".
  - `HOMES.gorge` stays `row([], false)`. Part 4 makes it pickable.
  - Owner question 1.
  - Cost if wrong: leaving it hidden is a revert of one model branch.
- **G11. Birds.**
  - `createSkyLife(scene, { rng, birdY })` takes the flock height, default 14, so other areas are unchanged. The Gorge passes `GORGE_BIRD_Y = 34`, above every rim and above `CAM_TOP`.
  - `gorgeLife` adds three wheeling birds over the gorge as one `InstancedMesh`, `noCast`. They fly circles of radius 9–14 m at y 32–36 and are driven by time only.
  - Cost if wrong: none to other areas.
- **G12. Ground lookups stay cheap.** The Gorge has about 60 shapes, and `ground.y` runs per actor per frame. Task 2 adds a 4 m bucket grid to `createGround`, used only with more than 16 shapes. It gives identical results (asserted on a 0.25 m grid with and without it). This is part 1's "first optimisation", written into `src/ground.js` after part 1 has merged.
  - Cost if wrong: none to behaviour.

- **G13. Colliders stay off rims.** Part 1's R3 asks for this. Every Gorge collider (tors, the Summit Rock, pines) keeps 1.5 m or more between its circle and any rim, lip or wall foot. So a cat can always pass, and never stops dead against a collider on an edge.
  - Cost if wrong: a cat jams at a rim; Task 6's test catches it.
- **G14. A one-way post to Twolegplace.** The stream leaves the south edge toward Twolegplace, and the spec's list says "posts back to the Moor and Twolegplace", so borderposts' R1 wants a post there. `post('gorge-town', -5, 47.5, 'south', 'neighborhood')` stands beside a floor path carried to the south edge. It has no twin: owner decision 10 says no post from Twolegplace, which is the `river-town-long` precedent.
  - Cost if wrong: if the owner wants no way out to the town, delete one post and one path segment.
