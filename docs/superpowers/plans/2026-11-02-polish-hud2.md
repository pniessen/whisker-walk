# polish-hud2: phone HUD and toasts

Survey 2, Build A1 (broken #1, #3, polish #11). Files: `src/ui/hud.js`,
`test/hud.test.js`, and the HUD/touch/toast/chat sections of `src/style.css`.
No 3D, no world builds, no walk logic: the flat trace and draw pins cannot move.

**Goal.** On a phone, the 💬 chat button no longer covers the 📷 button; toasts
never pile up over the cat or leave ghost text; the rank title is never cut off.

## Rulings

- **R1 Chat button on touch** joins the zoom row under the action cluster, as a
  third 44 px round button to the left of + and − (mirrored left-handed), not on
  the cluster. Both sides stay out of the joystick zone (left/right 40%). The
  tray opens above it, fixed to the screen, clamped to the screen width.
  Desktop (no touch) keeps its spot at right 16 / bottom 96 (no cluster there).
  Cost if wrong: one CSS rule pair to move it again.
- **R2 Toasts: at most 2 on screen.** A third toast retires the oldest at once
  (a short 0.35 s fade). Fades are shorter (0.35 s, a small rise) so a fading
  toast's text never outlives its pill as "ghost text".
- **R3 Spotted critters merge.** A critter discovery (`type: 'critter'`, new or
  repeat) that lands while the newest toast on screen is a critter toast folds
  into it: "You spotted 3 critters +15 🐾", and that toast's clock restarts.
  Other types never merge. Cost if wrong: one kid misses which critter it was;
  the journal still records each.
- **R4 Phone portrait toasts go to the top**, under the map/objective band,
  growing downward, away from the cat (which the camera keeps mid-screen).
  Only on touch at max-width 480 px and taller than 500 px; tablets, desktop and
  landscape keep their bottom anchors (with the cap of 2 they no longer climb
  into the cat). Cloud-sync toasts (#cloud-toasts) keep their place.
- **R5 Rank on phones** moves to a second line under the points pill (smaller
  pill, trophy kept), and the goals list steps down to make room. Ellipsis stays
  only as a last fallback.

## Task 1: toast cap, merge and fade (hud.js + CSS + tests)

- `hud.toast(text, points, opts)` keeps its signature; add internal list of live
  toasts, `MAX_TOASTS = 2`, retire-oldest, and an optional `opts.merge` key used
  by the discovery listener for critters.
- Discovery listener: critters pass a merge key; the merged text is
  `You spotted N critters  +P 🐾`.
- CSS: `.toast` transition 0.35 s opacity + transform; `.toast.fade` rises 6 px;
  reduced motion: no transform. Phone portrait (R4) top anchor for `#hud-toasts`.
- Tests (fake timers): cap at 2, oldest retired first, critter merge and count,
  non-critter does not merge, merge restarts the clock, CSS pins for R4.

## Task 2: chat button and rank on phones (CSS + tests)

- R1 rules for `body.touch-mode .chat-wheel` and the `(pointer: coarse)` twin,
  plus left-handed mirror; tray fixed above the zoom row.
- R5 rules in the `max-width: 480px` block: `.hud-top` stacks, `.hud-rank`
  smaller; `.hud-goals` steps down.
- CSS pin tests.
