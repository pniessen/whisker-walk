# The Reef (ocean dive): design and plan

Status: **for owner approval (2026-11-26). Nothing here is built.** One file holds the short design and the milestone plan; the Moon needed two, the Reef needs fewer pages because the Moon, the gates and the suit kit already paid for the machinery.
Builds on: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-suit-dive-moon-design.md` (branch `suit-dive-moon-design`, 63d68c8a: section 5 is the first Reef sketch; its owner answers were adopted 2026-11-22), `2026-11-23-teleport-gates-design.md` (the gate, merged), `2026-11-23-moon-design.md` and `plans/2026-11-23-moon.md` (the pattern this copies: flag, pure plan file, lazy chunk, staged wings, repeat job).
Every number marked "start" is a starting value the builder tunes from pictures.

## 0. Summary for a parent (half a page)

**The Reef is a big, bright, friendly place under the sea.** The cat walks to the foot of the Seaside pier, finds a glowing rock cleft with a ladder and a ring buoy, steps through, and a diving suit puts itself on her. She lands on a sandy seabed full of kelp, coral, shoals of fish and a slow turtle. She can walk the sand, or **hold the kick button to float up** (to about two metres) and drift over the coral. Sunlight wobbles down from above.

**There is a small story.** A little hermit crab called **Marlo** (a placeholder name, the daughter names him, as with the Lighthouse) has outgrown his shell and is too shy to go and look for a new one. The cat helps him try three shells (too small, too big, just right), each hidden somewhere different, and ends with a **housewarming**: fish, the turtle and a huge, gentle manta ray glide past the new shell and the coral lights up. Nobody is scary, nothing is timed, nothing can go wrong. A gentle **air-bubble gauge** exists, but running out only means a warm bubble lifts her to the ladder with nothing lost, and air pockets are everywhere.

**It is as big as the Moon's base wing** (about 96 x 84 m of seabed), built in **stages** so the game is never too heavy for an older iPad. It has six discoveries, three golden mice (two only reachable by floating), a found outfit (the **Pearl Collar**), shells to collect, and a reason to come back: **Marlo's errand of the day**, 25 paws once a walk, and an album of twelve reef postcards. It works in both editions (the Clan words only add light flavour, never ancestors).

**The deep wing (the Sunken Boat, the Old Anchor and the Glow Cave) is in the first release** (owner, 2026-11-26). One thing still needs your decision (section 9): which story.

## 3. The story and the mission

### 3.1 Three story options

| | Story | Why it fits | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Marlo's new shell (recommended)** | Shy hermit crab, three shells to try, a housewarming parade | Gentle, short steps; teaches float, walking the sand, air pockets and carrying; every creature gets a job in the ending | Carrying a shell means carry code (one item in the mouth; the shell returns to its spot if she leaves) |
| B. The dim coral | The glow-coral has gone pale; carry three light-plankton jars to relight it | Pretty ending | It is the Lighthouse's lamp job again |
| C. The sea-glass song | Gather bottles and ring them in order to wake a sleeping whale | Music, new | Needs an audio puzzle and is hard on mute, TV and pad |

### 3.2 Cast and tone

**Marlo**, a small hermit crab with an over-tight, cracked shell, waves a claw, shows picture bubbles (a shell, a question mark, a heart) rather than text, follows her at a shy distance and waits. The **meow button calls him**: a ring of bubbles shows the way to the next goal (no text, no fail). The **manta**, **Sela the turtle** (placeholder) and the fish school are only ever friendly. Nothing is dark, lonely or scary. The Glow Cave glows; there is no void.

### 3.3 Mission (seven steps, three chapters)

| # | Chapter | Step | Skills taught |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 Shallows (shell A) | **Meet Marlo** on the sand by the gate. Interact. He shows a cracked shell and a picture of a new one. | Interact |
| 2 | 1 | **Shell one, the too-small shell**, in the Kelp Garden (walk the sand, find the glint). Carry it to Marlo (it is too small; he sighs). | carry, walk |
| 3 | 1 | **Shell two, the too-big shell**, on a coral ledge at 1.4 m, reachable only by floating. | float |
| 4 | 2 Coral Garden (wing B) | **Shell three, the just-right shell**, inside a shallow cave behind the coral, ringed by an air pocket (teaches that air pockets refill the gauge). | air pockets |
| 5 | 2 | **Try it on.** Marlo steps in; a happy wobble; the old shell is left as a little home for a sea snail. | |
| 6 | 3 Housewarming | **Light the coral.** Touch the three lantern corals along a path; they glow in turn. | |
| 7 | 3 | **The parade.** The manta and the shoal glide past (a pure function of one start time, 20 s, shared in a room). The **Pearl Collar's pearl** is given; `reefstory` pays once (40 paws). | |

Progress is saved by step (`state.reef.step`, `shells` bits); a carried shell goes back to its spot at walk end and is never lost or sent to the den. Chapter 3 is the wing the first release can cut without hiding anything.

### 3.4 After the story

**Marlo's errand of the day**, picked from the room seed (the same for a co-walk), 25 paws once a walk: (1) bring him three sea-glass pieces, (2) follow a school through a ring of kelp, (3) find the shell that changed colour, (4) float through five bubble rings in order, (5) carry a small hat shell to a sleepy snail, (6) a lost glove at one of six spots. Each pays a **reef postcard** into an album of twelve. Also repeatable: shells and sea-glass (8 collectibles), the six discoveries, three mice, one found outfit a walk.

## 4. The place

### 4.1 Size and shape

Walkable bounds start at `{ minX -48, maxX 48, minZ -42, maxZ 42 }` = 96 x 84 m. North is -z. The seabed is gently dune-rolled (heights up to 1.2 m, steepest slope under 0.5) on one smooth function, like the Moon's `moonY`, drawn to 130 m with a soft fog so it never ends in a wall. **No `waters`** (the surface is a shimmer ceiling, not a water body).

### 4.2 Zones

| Zone | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| **The Ladder (spawn)** | (0, 36) | the gate cleft's return arch (a coral arch in the Reef's own style, a mirror of the host look), a ladder and ring buoy hanging down from the shimmer ceiling, Marlo on the sand, a small locker-sign picture, the first air pocket |
| **Kelp Garden** | (-24, 16) | tall instanced kelp that sways, a seahorse, sea-glass, shell one |
| **Coral Garden** (wing B) | (16, 0) | clumps at three heights, the ledge (shell two), the shallow cave (shell three), the lantern corals, the school |
| **Sandy Flats** | (-8, -14) | open sand, turtle, crabs, the postcard board, the Treasure Chest |
| **Deep wing** (C, first release) | (-32, -34) | the Sunken Boat, the Old Anchor, the Glow Cave (a small rock cleft lit by plankton), the third air-pocket chain |

### 4.3 Look

Warm blue-green exponential fog (start near 6 m, far 40 m), a shimmer ceiling quad at y 9 (2 triangles, scrolled texture), three additive light-shaft quads, sand with ripple colouring in vertex colours, instanced kelp (sway in the vertex shader or a single scaled update, never per-blade meshes), merged coral by material, one decal atlas for fish and posters, no real caustics (a scrolled floor texture). Tiers by quality rung (LOW/LITE drop the small fish and half the shafts, as the Moon dropped stars). Reduced motion stops the scroll and the sway.

## 5. Movement, breath, camera

- **`placephysics.js` row** `reef: { medium: 'water', pace: 0.7, floatMax: 2.2, rise: 1.2, sink: 0.6 }` (start). The Moon's reading sites multiply what they compute, so nothing else changes; a test pins that the table has exactly two rows and that every other place's trace (`flattrace*`, `growtrace*`) is byte-unchanged.
- **`floatY`** is a new read-only height from the seabed. It feeds only `checkFind` (the golden-mouse window 0.9 m) and found-outfit pick-ups (`catReaches`), exactly as `perchY` does for a perched cat. It never feeds `perchY`, `climbBudget` or any collider, and a test fails if anything else reads it. Held kick: rises at `rise` 1.2 m/s (eased 0.2 s); released: sinks at `sink` 0.6 m/s. Below 0.3 m she walks the sand (existing stalk/walk), above it she paddles (the existing swim pose, tail streaming). The pounce hop does not run here; resting, naps, grooming and carrying-by-mouth stay (carrying is the story), the rest are off as for a swimming cat.
- **Pad** A is the held kick; the stick holds float height by position (no new axis). **Touch** gets a big kick button in the pounce slot (and a second stacked "down" is not needed: release sinks). **Keyboard** Space.
- **Air gauge:** five bubbles; 90 s from full; hidden the first 20 s of a first visit; at two bubbles a soft line and a ring around the pack; at zero a warm bubble lifts her to the ladder (fade-and-place with reduced motion), the gauge refills at once, nothing lost, no sound but a soft chime. A property test shows there is no path to a failure state, and a phrase scan bans *drown, die, dead, hurt, panic, gasp, choke, scary* from every Reef string.
- **Camera:** the chase camera as is, with distance 4.0, a floor clamp (camera y no lower than seabed + 0.4) and a 60 percent follow of `floatY` so a kick does not whip the view; `camzoom` keeps working.
- **Strays, critters, ghosts, weather, rivals, the yard dog, daily goals and clan cats are off in the Reef** (the Moon's M1 live check found these leak in unless the place says no: do it in M0).

## 10. Risks

1. **Start-up chunk** (about 11 kB left). Everything big is lazy; the seams are measured; a milestone over its share does not merge.
2. **Two kinds of height** (`perchY`, `floatY`). `floatY` is read only by finds and pick-ups; a test fails if anything else reads it.
3. **Transparent draws on phones** (shafts, the shimmer ceiling, the helmet). Few and small; measured on iPhone 18 in R1, not first discovered in R9.
4. **Water drift.** Two ways to be in water (the Seaside's wading, the Reef's float). The Reef has no `waters`, so the swim code never runs there.
5. **Mood.** Underwater can read as lonely or scary; warm light, friendly creatures, no darkness, no failing meter, a banned-word scan.
6. **The shared carry code.** A carried shell is a mission item and must return to its spot rather than go to the den like a keepsake (the Moon's pattern); a test covers every way a walk can end with a shell in her mouth.
7. **Edition drift.** A new line in one set only; the existing parity tests run over every new id.
8. **Seaside budget.** It is the tightest host; the gate is the only thing added, and R6 re-measures before merge.
