# The Moon: plan

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-moon-design.md` (design for owner approval; nothing is built). Concept pictures: `verify-moon.html` (`?shot=earthrise|crater-rover|launchpad|layout`).
**Rule for every milestone:** its own branch off main in a worktree under `.worktrees/`, pictures for owner sign-off where the look changes, the full suite and `npm run build` green, start-up growth within the Moon's 1.0 kB total (the ceiling stays 720 kB, never raised), the chunk at most **94 kB gz** (the build's 100 stays) and built triangles at most **56,000**, no ceiling raised in `test/budget.test.js` for an existing place, one Chrome at a time. Merge and push only after the owner's yes (or the standing autonomous-merge permission if he gives it for this work).
**Builder model:** Sonnet builds, Haiku for look-ups and tests, Opus only for design questions and the final review (owner's routing note). One or two builds at a time.
**Sizes:** S = under a day, M = one to two days, L = three or more days (of one Sonnet builder). "Chunk" is the Moon's gzip size after the milestone (start numbers, re-measured and pinned).

## Order and how it meets the gate plan

Moon M0 to M4 are built behind `?moon=1` (`moonflag.js`, as the Lighthouse and Greenhouse were) and reached by a developer shortcut; they do **not** wait for the gates. Needs by milestone:

| Moon milestone | Needs |
|---|---|
| M0 seams | the merged suit kit (`placephysics.js`, `suithook.js`): yes, on main |
| M1, M2 | the suit look for the Moon (`LOOK_OF_PLACE.moon = 'space'`): suit M0 is on main |
| M4 (stage A complete) | nothing; **the gate plan's M3 (Moon hand-off) waits for M4** and adds the gate end, arrival and suit look |
| gate M0 to M2 (spike, art, suit auto-wear) | none of the Moon: they run in parallel |
| gate M5 (map marks) | any time after M4 |
| M5 onward | M4 merged; wings are independent of each other except M7 after M6 |

One change to the gate plan: its M3 row set grows by `roofs-moon` (everyday edition, `archway`, `needWalks 2`) beside `pines-moon`, and its table test accepts two hosts for one destination (spec Q1). If the owner declines the second gate, the everyday edition simply cannot reach the Moon and the Moon's `edition` stays both only for the Clan side.

- **The spike answered: an `InstancedMesh` survives everything.** `mergeStaticProps` leaves a top-level mesh alone, `assignShadowCasters` treats it as one thing (its box spans every instance, so it is eligible: medium, large and boulders cast, the small rocks carry `userData.noCast`), `trimShadowCasters` keeps it as one caster, and the contact-shadow scan needs `userData.noContactShadow` (without it the plain's rocks would get one huge footprint; the ground and the four rock meshes carry it). Rocks are four instanced meshes (70 / 40 / 20 / 10 boulders), no merging per cell. Cost to know: the shadow pass always draws all of a class (frustum culling sees one bounding sphere); the boulders (the only ones with colliders) and the medium and large rocks are 70 instances of 20 to 80 triangles.
- **The ground is not `createGround`'s strips and discs.** Those are a MAX over shapes and cannot dig a bowl. The Moon's own ground (`createMoonGround` in `moonplan.js`) answers the walk's interface (`y`, `ceil`, `canStep`, `slide`, `sameLevel`, `gradient`, `tilt`) from one smooth function, `moonY`: six cosine bowls (the deepest bowl at a point, so the twin bowls do not double their slope) with a low rim, the Earthrise Ridge, and a wall of low hills round the 11 to 15 m beyond the walkable bounds so the 130 m ground never ends in a visible edge. Steepest slope inside the bounds 0.51 (the twin bowls' crease); the walk's step rule allows nearly 1. The drawn mesh is one 6,496-triangle indexed grid (2 m across the plain, coarser in the hills) in the game's gravel tile with vertex colours for the mottle, the darker bowls and the lighter rims. LOW/LITE do not halve the grid (the tiers drop the small rocks, half the stars, the dust and the cloud drift); the grid is 6.5k triangles either way.
- **Ridges:** only the Earthrise Ridge is in the ground (walkable, 3.2 m, 0.42 at the steepest). Skip Ridge, Star Hill, the Viewing Mound and the Cave Massif come with the milestones that build on them (their heights are the ground's to add then).
- **Landing site:** the lander (a drum with a 3.0 m deck, four legs, a hatch, an antenna and dish), a 1.0 m crate and a 2.1 m ledge (the deck, the ledge and the crate are `perches`, no labels; on the Moon's budget the deck is one hop from the dust, on Earth's it is the chain only), the flag (6, 38), four landing lights, and the return gate ring (-10, 41) with five stones, a lintel, a soft disc and a faint column of light; **inert** (no gate row, nothing happens at it). Spawn (0, 45). Ten boulders are colliders. 12 wander spots (`pois`): the walk's secrets read them and a place with none crashes the walk at its start (a find of this milestone's live check; milestone 0's empty list did).
- **The sun is the game's own** (`render/rig.js`'s fixed south-west direction), not the spec's low sun in the east: Earth's lit side is the left, the night side the right, and the picture-page concept's east sun is not reproduced. A per-place sun would be a shared-code change; none was made.
- **Earth** is one 256 px canvas quad (40 m at 275 m, 8.3 degrees, 21 degrees up, 12 degrees east of north) and a 28-triangle cloud disc that drifts (still on the lite rung and in reduced motion), 700 stars (350 lite) as one Points. Hemisphere fill raised from M0's 0.34 to 0.55 so the sides away from the sun are not pitch black.
- **Camera ease** 60 percent (`CAMERA_FOLLOW`), the **skip** (0.1 m a stride, through `session.hopLift`, the Market's shoo hop's own channel) and the **landing dust** (10 puffs, 5 on lite, `fx.burst` with ttl 1.2 s and a tenth of the gravity) are in `world/moonlife.js` and run after `player.update` like every area life; the dust fires on the frame `game/avatar.js` plays the thump (a test runs both against one clock). Not done: the quieter, filtered "pff" for the landing sound (`audio.js` is start-up code; the ordinary thump plays), and "no drag" (`fx.burst` has no drag to turn off; the puffs fall at a tenth of gravity and live 1.2 s).

**Built (branch `moon-m3`, 2026-11-24), what differs from the list above:**
- **Start-up: +0 bytes** (the same 952 B over main as M2: main 826b6458 651,343 B gz, this branch 652,295). Every source file is a `src/world/moon*.js`; three are new (`moonrover.js`, `moonroverlive.js`, `moonroverparts.js`); `fairnone.js`, `main.js`, `walk.js`, `interactions.js`, `avatar.js` and `player.js` are untouched (a test pins that none of them names the rover). **Moon chunk 29.7 kB gz** (29,736 B; M2 23,237; plan row 46, ceiling 100).
- **How it hooks in with no start-up code.** An area life is given no player and no stick, and the Fairground's carried-seat model is the one the spec names. So on its first frame the life swaps the walk's `session.fair` (NO_FAIR away from the Fairground) for a copy of it with four calls of its own (`update`, `late`, `jump`, `pawKind`), which main.js already calls every frame with the player; `dispose` puts the walk's own back. The seat is a perch record (`pose: 'requestPet'`, `speedFactor` 0, `perchHold` every frame so a pushed stick does not take her off), so **no new cat triangles and no cat-cap exposure** (a test pins that Dot's files import nothing of the cat). Verified in the real game page (`?moon=1`, the built walk): the hook is installed, "E - hop in Dot" shows at 2.9 m, one press seats her, W drives, "E - hop out" shows, one press puts her on free ground and Dot is a collider again.
- **Free steering shipped** (`ROVER_FREE = true` in `moonrover.js`); the **Rover Tour** is built and tested as the one-constant fallback (hop in as usual, the stick does nothing, Dot drives the 209 m loop at 6 m/s from the nearest track point; `tourPoint(s)` is a pure function of the distance along the loop; the corners are cut with 4.5 m fillets). Owner's hand check (iPhone and a pad) decides; nothing here has been felt on a device.
- **The sim** (`moonrover.js`, pure): a 1.3 m circle, 7 m/s, 5 m/s2 up and 8 down, 2.2 rad/s; slower while pointed away from the wanted way (a quarter of its speed at worst) and slower up a slope steeper than 0.28 (30 percent of the speed left at 0.58: never stopped, never flipped); pushed out of every collider and the bounds (inset 1 m); a step that would leave it inside one (a pinch) is not taken; a start inside one is first moved to the nearest free ground. Properties tested over many random streams and seeds: never in a collider, never out of the bounds, deterministic, a way off at every moment (`dismountSpot`: free of every collider by a cat's margin and inside the bounds from 3,000 random places and the corners, her side first). The steepest ground on the track is a 0.25 grade.
- **The stick is the walk's own, turned by the camera, and the camera never swings by itself** (deviation from "7 m behind"): a camera that follows the heading turns the stick's meaning under her thumb (the Rooftops' balance walk needs a held world direction to avoid it). The walk's camera is carried with her, eased out 45 percent and up 0.55 m while she drives, looking 1.4 m ahead at speed; zoom steps, L1/R1 and the drag still work. Owner decision if a chase camera is wanted.
