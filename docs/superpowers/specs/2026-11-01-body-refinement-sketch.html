Fluffy cat: the ruff

  • Today: a smooth white ring all the way round the neck, like a life preserver.
  • New: soft tufts, thickest under the chin and down the chest, thinning to nothing at the back of the neck.
  • The tufts are coat-coloured on the sides and blend into the white chest at the front.

Cat legs and feet

  • Each leg is one smooth piece: no seams at the joints. Front legs grow out of the shoulders and taper to a slim wrist.
  • Back legs bend like a real cat's: a strong thigh, the knee forward, the hock back, then a slim ankle (no cankles).
  • Each foot is one rounded shape with four toes scalloped into the front and small claws just showing.

Hagrid

  • A neck joins his head to his body, and his body has a full breast and a back that rises to the tail.
  • A fan of long, curved tail feathers instead of three discs.
  • Wings folded along his sides, tapering to feather tips at the back.
  • One bumpy comb with points, two hanging wattles, and a slightly hooked beak.
  • Feathery thighs, scaly yellow legs, three toes forward and one back, with claw tips.
