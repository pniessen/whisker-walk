# Controller in the menus: hand-check

For the owner, with a DualSense (CFI-ZCT2W) on the laptop. Plug it in (or pair it by Bluetooth), open the game, and press any button once. You should see "Controller connected".

Do this with the sound off in the game's Settings if you like; nothing here needs sound.

## What the buttons do

In a walk (unchanged):

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Left stick | Walk |
| Right stick | Look around |
| Cross (X mark) | Pounce |
| Circle | Meow |
| Square | Interact |
| Triangle | Camera |
| L1 / R1 | Zoom out / in |
| Options | Pause |

In menus, cards and the map (new):

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Left stick or D-pad | Move the white ring to the next control |
| Cross | Press the control (button, switch, picture). On a dropdown it picks the next choice |
| Circle | Back (one step out, like the Back buttons) |
| Left / Right on a slider | Changes the slider one notch |
| Right stick | Scrolls a long card. On the world map it slides the map |
| L1 / R1 | On the world map: zoom out / in |
| Options | In the pause card, carry on exploring. In a walk, pause |

The white ring only shows after you use the pad. Touch the mouse or screen and it goes away again.

## What to try, screen by screen

Tick each one. Write down anything odd.

- [ ] **Home base.** Move around: cat pictures, Dress up, today's place, the news line, Explore, Menu. Cross on Menu opens it. Ring is easy to see.
- [ ] **Menu.** Reach My clan, Journal, Album, Skills, My den, Sound & controls, For grown-ups (small, faint line near the bottom). Circle goes back to the home screen.
- [ ] **Sound & controls.** Sliders change with left/right. Switches flip with Cross. The dropdown (Map) changes with Cross. Careful: Mute switches the game's sound off or on.
- [ ] **For grown-ups.** Game settings, Gift code, Replay lesson all reachable. Circle backs out of each.
- [ ] **Pause card** (Options in a walk). Resume, sound switches, view size, Head home all reachable. Options resumes.
- [ ] **Ready? card** (start of a walk). Press down once, then Cross. Start exploring needs you to pick it first; see "Known gaps".
- [ ] **End-of-walk card (Continue).** Press down once, then Cross.
- [ ] **Dress up.** Pick a slot, pick an item, Try on. Circle once closes the list, a second time goes home. Done works.
- [ ] **Make a cat** (the dotted picture in the cat row). Reach the colour swatches, the shapes and Done. Circle leaves.
- [ ] **World map** (Change place). Move from place to place; the map slides to follow. Right stick slides the map when it is zoomed in. L1/R1 zoom. Cross on a place you have unlocked asks "Are you sure?". Cross on a locked place shows how many walks are left.
- [ ] **Confirm card** ("Are you sure?"). Press Cross at once: it should say wait. Wait a moment, press Cross again: it goes ahead.
- [ ] **Den shop** (walk into the den, the sheet that opens). Reach the items and Close. Circle closes.
- [ ] **Visit celebration** (on a visit milestone). Its button is reachable.
- [ ] **Mouse and touch.** Click or tap around: no white ring anywhere, everything looks as before.
- [ ] **Hand-off.** Start a walk with the pad, pause, resume, go home, with no stuck buttons and nothing pressed twice.

## Known gaps (checked in the browser with a pretend pad, 2026-11-21)

1. Some screens open with nothing selected: the Ready? card, the end-of-walk card, My den, Make a cat. The first stick push selects something nearby, not always the main button. On the Ready? card, one push down went straight to "Skip the lesson"; push up to get to Start exploring. (Cross does nothing until something is selected.) Options (Start) does start the walk from the Ready? card.
2. On Sound & controls, Down from the Back button skips the Mute / Left-handed / Invert / Zoom switches (they are wide rows). They are reachable, but not in the order you would expect: go Down to Explore at the bottom, then Up. Left and Right on a dropdown do nothing (they only move sliders).
3. Circle does nothing on the Ready? and pause cards. Use Cross on Head home or Options to resume.
4. Text boxes (friend code, cat name, gift code) are skipped by the pad on purpose; use the keyboard.
5. The cat in the middle of home base and the cat builder cannot be turned with the pad. Picking only.
6. My clan, Album and Journal show only Back, Home and a few buttons. The lists are pictures only; there is nothing to press in them.
7. Not seen at all in the browser, so only read in the code: the news, notice, nearby and clan-moment cards on home base, the confirm card's wait, the visit celebration, and the den shop (they need a real save to reach). Please look at these first.

## Open questions

- Does the iPhone/iPad app pass a controller through? This is UNVERIFIED. The app uses the iOS web view, and it may not give the page the controller. Needs a real iPad/iPhone with a controller paired.
- Stick feel: how long before a held stick repeats, and how fast. I could not feel this; it is set to 0.4 s then every 0.14 s. Tell me too fast or too slow.
- Is the ring easy enough to see on every screen, especially the world map?
- Should the first push on a fresh screen always land on the main button (gap 1)? I left it as is because fixing it safely touches the focus code.
- Safari and the app's web view may handle focus slightly differently from Chrome. Needs a real device.
