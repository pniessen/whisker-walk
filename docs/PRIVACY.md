# Whisker Walk privacy policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Whisker Walk is a small family game about walking a cat. It is run by [NAME OR "the game's owner"], not by a company. This page says what the game keeps, where it keeps it, and how to have it removed.

## The short version

- You can play the whole game without giving it anything about yourself. It never asks for your real name, your age, an email address or a password.
- The game has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
- Your game is saved on your own device. It only goes online if you choose to use **Cloud save**, **Play together** or the **Colony**.
- The only thing you type that other players can see is your cat's name. Please pick a cat name, not your real name.
- You can ask for anything stored online to be deleted. See "Deleting your data".

## What stays on your device

The game saves these in your browser's storage (or inside the app on an iPhone or iPad). They do not leave the device unless you turn on one of the online features below.

- Your progress: points, walks, what your cat owns and wears, your journal, skills, den and colony.
- Your album: small pictures the game takes of the game world. The game never uses your camera or your photo library.
- Your settings, and a count of how often each screen has been opened.
- Backup copies of your save, kept so a mistake can be undone.
- A random id and a random secret that the game makes up for this device. They do not come from your name, your account or your device's serial number.
- The list of players you have met, and the list of visitors you have hidden.

Clearing the browser's site data, or deleting the app, removes all of it from that device.

## What goes online, and when

Nothing in this section happens unless you use the feature named.

### Cloud save

When you tap **Save to cloud**, the game makes up a code (three words and a number) and uploads a copy of your save and your album under that code. After that it uploads again at the end of each walk. Stored online:

- the save and the album, exactly as they are on your device;
- the code, a secret that proves later uploads come from your device, your total points, and the time of the last upload.

Anyone who has your code can load a copy of your save. Treat the code like a password, and do not post screenshots of the Cloud save screen. **Unlink** stops further uploads from that device; it does not delete the copy already stored online.

### Play together and friends

When you save a cat name, host or join a shared walk, or pair a colony, the game uploads a small profile so other players can see your cat. Stored online:

- your cat's name, its breed and look, what it is wearing, and your rank title;
- your device's random id, its secret, and the time you were last seen.

When two players greet each other in a shared walk, or add each other with a friend code, the game stores that those two ids are friends, how many times they have greeted, and when.

Your friend code is made from your device's random id. Share it only with people you know.

### During a shared walk

While you walk with someone, your device sends theirs where your cat is, what it is doing, and its name and look. Chat is a fixed list of phrases and emoji chosen from a wheel; the game cannot send typed messages. These messages pass through the game's online service to the other players in the room and are not stored by the game.

A room is joined with a four-character code. Anyone who has the code can join that room, so share room codes only with the people you mean to walk with.

### The colony

If two players pair their colonies, the game stores the shared colony online: the stray cats in it (their game names and where they were found), which player found, fed, tamed or dressed each one, the accessories each player can trade, and trade offers between the two.

### Finding a player in the same room (iPhone and iPad app only)

If you allow it, the app uses your local network to notice another device running Whisker Walk nearby, so you can join their walk without typing a code. It shares the room code and the cat's name with nearby devices running the app. It does not use your location, and iOS asks your permission first.

## What the game never collects

- Real names, ages, birthdays, email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses.
- Location.
- Photos, camera, microphone or contacts.
- Advertising ids, or anything used to follow you across other apps or sites.
- Payment details. Nothing in the game costs money.

## Other services involved

The game relies on a few services, and each one can see ordinary technical details of a connection, such as an IP address and the time.

- **Supabase** stores the online data described above and passes messages during shared walks.
- **GitHub Pages** serves the website version of the game.
- **Google Fonts** supplies the lettering on one page, the "Clan Edition" announcement page. The game itself does not load it.
- **Apple** delivers the iPhone and iPad app.

The game's owner does not sell or share anyone's data, and does not use it for anything other than making the game work.

## Children

Whisker Walk is made for children and is meant to be set up by a parent. It is built so that a child has nothing personal to give it: there are no accounts, no typed chat and no profile beyond a cat's name. The one risk is a child typing a real name as the cat's name, so the game reminds them not to, limits names to letters, and checks them again on the server.

Parents: you can see what is stored for your child by opening the game on their device, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time.

## How long data is kept

Online data is kept until it is deleted on request. It is not removed automatically.

## Deleting your data

- **On a device:** use **Start over** in Game settings, or clear the site's data in the browser, or delete the app.
- **Online:** write to [CONTACT] with your cloud save code, or your friend code, or both. The owner will delete the matching save, profile, friend links and colony records by hand. The game does not yet have a button that does this for you.

## Security

Saves and profiles can only be changed by a device that holds the matching secret. The online database refuses direct reading of saves. No system is perfectly secure, which is one more reason the game is designed to hold nothing personal.
