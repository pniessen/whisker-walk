# Watching a child play: a ten-minute script

This is for a grown-up to use with one child at a time. It checks the findings of the expert review against a real player. It takes about ten minutes per child.

## Before you start

- Use the device the child normally plays on, with the newest build. In Game settings the last line says which build it is.
- Sit beside them where you can see the screen and their hands.
- Have this page and a pen, or a notes app. Make one copy of the table per child.
- Say this, and then nothing else: **"I'm checking the game, not you. There are no wrong answers. Tell me what you're thinking as you go. I can't help, because I want to see where the game is confusing."**

## The rules for you

These matter more than the tasks.

1. **Don't help, hint or point.** If they ask "how do I…?", say "what would you try?" Count to ten in your head before saying anything at all.
2. **Don't explain the game** afterwards either, until both children have had their turn.
3. **Write what they do, not what you think.** "Tapped the picture twice, then scrolled" is useful. "Seemed confused" is not.
4. **Give up on a task after about a minute.** Say "that's fine, let's try the next one." A task they can't finish is the most useful result you can get.
5. **One child at a time,** with the other out of the room. The second child shouldn't have watched the first.

## What to note for each task

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ | Did it straight away |
| ~ | Got there after a wrong turn, a pause of more than about five seconds, or a question |
| ✗ | Gave up, or you stopped the task |

Also write down, in their words if you can: the first thing they tapped, anything they tapped that did nothing, anything they read aloud wrongly or skipped, and anything they said.

## The tasks

Read each one aloud exactly as written. They are worded to avoid naming the button.

| # | Say this | What it checks | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Take your cat out for a walk somewhere you haven't been lately." | Changing place, and starting | Do they find the place card? Do they expect a Start button on the map screen? |
| 2 | "While you're walking, tell me what each of those round buttons does." | Icon-only walk buttons | Which ones they guess wrong, especially the cat face. |
| 3 | "Something just popped up on the screen. What did it say?" (Ask right after any message appears.) | Whether messages stay long enough to read | Could they read all of it? Did it vanish first? |
| 4 | "Go back home." | Ending a walk | Do they find pause, then end? Do they swipe from the edge of the screen instead? |
| 5 | "Put something new on your cat that it isn't wearing now." | Dress up, and buying by accident | Do they mean to buy, or does it happen while trying things on? What do they do when the "Get it?" card appears? |
| 6 | "Show me every kind of animal you've found so far." | Finding the Journal | First place they look. |
| 7 | "Show me a photo you took." | Finding the Album | First place they look. Is it empty, and do they know how to fill it? |
| 8 | "You want to walk with your brother or sister. Get as far as you can without them." | Play together, the name box, the codes | Do they understand the cat-name step? Can they say what each code box is for? |
| 9 | "How many points do you need for the next rank? And what's a golden mouse?" | Whether the Menu's numbers make sense | Do they read the top line of the Menu, and can they explain it? |
| 10 | "Make the music quieter." | Finding settings, and the sliders | Which menu they open first. Can they drag the slider at the first try? |

If the child already has a made cat, add: **"Change your cat's name."** Watch whether they go to the cat builder or to Play together; the game has a name in each.

## Three questions at the end

Ask these after the tasks, and write the answers down word for word.

1. "What's your favourite thing to do in the game?"
2. "Is there anything that's annoying, or that you never bother with?"
3. "If you could add one thing, what would it be?"

## What to do with the notes

- **A ✗ or ~ that the expert review also flagged:** fix it. Two sources agree.
- **A ✗ or ~ the review missed:** the most valuable kind. Write down exactly what happened.
- **Something the review flagged that both children sailed through:** leave it alone.
- **Answers to question 2:** compare with the "What gets used" table in Game settings. A screen neither child mentions and the table shows unopened is a candidate to simplify or remove.

Two sessions with two children is a complete study for this game. Repeat it after any large change to the screens.
